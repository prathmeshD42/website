About Sona Plastics

A legacy of over 28 years in precision injection molding. Based in Pune, India, serving capacitor manufacturers, electronics OEMs, furniture brands, and automotive trim fabricators.

Our Heritage

Pioneering Capacitor Components Since 1996

Established in 1996 under the leadership of Mr. Satish Gaikwad, Sona Plastics was founded to address the Indian electronics sector's need for high-quality, domestically manufactured plastic capacitor enclosures and insulating liner blocks.

Over the decades, we developed an extensive tooling library of over 100 precision molds covering standard cylindrical capacitor cans (from 27mm to 55mm diameter), SQ cans with integrated mounting clamps, AL top sealing discs, and industrial plastic levelers.

Today, we have expanded our molding capabilities into specialized automotive interior trims and HVAC components, providing tier suppliers with agile, low-to-medium batch manufacturing, fast mold adaptation, and zero-defect quality control.
