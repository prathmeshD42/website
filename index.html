Company Profile

Engineering Reliability in Every Molded Component

Established in 1996 in Pune by Mr. Satish Gaikwad, Sona Plastics began operations with dedicated plastic components for the capacitor industry. Today, our advanced injection molding facility supplies robust electrical enclosures, mounting hardware, furniture components, and specialized automotive interior trims and HVAC air-handling parts.

We pride ourselves on tight dimensional accuracy, consistent surface aesthetics, and versatile polymer processing capabilities across standard and engineering grades.

Electronics Enclosures

Outer & inner capacitor cans, tops, and SQ clamp units.

Auto Trims & HVAC

Interior trims, vent louvers, ducting clips & brackets.

Polymer Range

Polypropylene (PP), ABS, HIPS, PC, and Polystyrene.

Flexible Batches

Capable of both specialized small batches & high-volume runs.

Plant & Technology

Manufacturing Infrastructure & Quality Standards

Our facility at Mohitewadi Bypass Road, Pune is equipped with modern injection molding machinery, temperature-controlled mold cooling, and precision metrology testing.

Injection Molding Machinery

Equipped with high-tonnage microprocessor-controlled injection molding machines for precise injection speed, pressure, and clamp rigidity ensuring zero warpage and flash-free parts.

  • Automated cycle parameter logging
  • Dehumidifying raw polymer hopper dryers
  • Mold temperature controllers & chillers

Polymer Material Expertise

We process both commodity and high-performance engineering plastics, compounding custom additives for flame retardancy, UV stabilization, and dielectric strength.

Polypropylene (PP) ABS Resins HIPS (High Impact) Polycarbonate (PC)

Quality Inspection & Testing

Stringent quality control protocols from raw material batch inspection to finished product dimensional testing and dielectric voltage breakdown screening.

  • Digital vernier & micrometer gauge checks
  • Dielectric insulation & wall thickness tests
  • Drop impact & thermal heat cycle checks
